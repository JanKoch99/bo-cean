# Bo@cean

## Introduction
It is a 2D puzzle platformer in which you need to control the environment (weather, terrain, ...) in order to move the character to the finish line. The character is on a boat in the ocean to discover new lands. On the way to the new lands, he must overcome different challenges and obstacl to succeed.
The game is designed for a single player.

## Tetrad
### Mechanics
- The boat is constantly moving, and the sails are pivoting from right to left 
- Actions: 
  - Blow the wind into the sail 
  - If you take the wind action, it gives the boat a boost where the sails are aligned to 
  - Create lightning which goes into the water to make a wave 
  - The boat rides the wave and gets pushed in a direction (left/right) 
  - Let it rain to raise the water level 
  - The ocean level rises and the boat can now move over obstacles 
### Aesthetics (graphics + sound)
- A simple style with a lot of water and a boat 
- Music should display the state of mind of the player (stressful, chill, ...) 

### History
- It is after World War 3 in the US (New York) and there is no food left. The character wants to flee to Europe and because there is no more power and technology available, he decides to go on his own with his self-made boat.
